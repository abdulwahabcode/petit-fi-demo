Our story

A hidden gem on historic George Street

Tucked along the cobbles of Hastings Old Town, Petit Fi is the little bistro with a big personality. Famously quirky, endlessly cosy, and completely dog-friendly — it's the seaside escape locals whisper about and visitors stumble upon with delight.

Everything is made with love in our tiny kitchen, but our true claim to fame is the cake counter: an ever-changing display of fresh homemade cakes, crowned by our legendary Basque burnt cheesecake — caramelised, gooey-centred, and worth the trip alone.

  • 🐶 Dogs very welcome — treats on the house
  • 🍰 Fresh homemade cakes baked daily
  • ☕ Proper coffee, two minutes from the seafront
